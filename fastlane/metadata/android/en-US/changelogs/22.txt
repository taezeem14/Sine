Sine v1.2.0 (Build 22):
• High-Fidelity Audio Engine: Restored rock-solid, instant audio streaming pipeline with Progressive Muxed MP4 (itag 18) priority and resilient fallback.
• Uninterrupted Background Playback: Permanent foreground service retention and Android Doze mode protection preventing playback halts when the screen is locked.
